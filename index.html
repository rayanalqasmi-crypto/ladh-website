About Us

Ladh.me is a local food concept created for people who love bold flavors and convenient, hassle-free meals. We focus on high-quality ingredients, fresh preparation, and creating dishes that delight the senses. Our mission is simple: make it easy for customers to enjoy their favorite meals by ordering online and picking them up directly from their car. Follow us on Instagram @ladh.me for daily specials, behind-the-scenes moments, and real-time updates.
